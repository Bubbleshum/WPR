**Your Windows Phone just got its Xbox LIVE back.** Well, sort of. Meet **WPR Hub**: a real online
home for your games, with a gamertag, a gamerscore, friends, messages and leaderboards. It's the
biggest thing WPR has ever shipped, so the version number jumped to **0.2** to celebrate.

**Sign in once and you have a gamertag.** Pick GitHub or Google, approve the key on the
hub's website, and choose your gamertag. That's it. No twelve-character Xbox name with three
underscores and a birth year. Unless you want one, of course.

**Every achievement you have ever unlocked in WPR counts.** None of them was ever sent anywhere
before, so the first time you sign in, your whole back catalogue goes up in one go. Your gamerscore
starts where you left off, not at zero. Watching it tick up is extremely satisfying.

**High scores go online.** Games that had Xbox LIVE leaderboards now post to real ones, and you can
see where you rank. Haven't signed in yet? Your scores wait for you and go up the moment you do.

**Friends, presence and messages.** Add friends by gamertag, see who's online and what they're
playing, look at what they've unlocked lately, and send them messages. Want to play in peace?
**Appear offline** on Android: you can still see and send messages, but nobody sees you.

**And when a game breaks, it can tell us.** Crash reports go to the hub automatically, so the
games that go wrong on your phone end up on our list without you having to file a bug.
**Play with logging** records a run from start to finish, and one button sends it with a report
code you can quote. It's a big help in getting your favourite broken game working.

> **What goes online, and when.** Nothing about your account leaves the device until you sign in.
> Crash reports and *play with logging* reports include the game's log and some details about your
> device, so we can tell what went wrong. When you install a game, WPR tells the hub which version
> you installed (a fingerprint of the file). If the hub doesn't have that version yet, it can ask
> for a copy, and with crash reports switched on WPR will upload it so we can test it. Turn
> **server logging** off in settings and none of that is sent. Achievements, scores and playtime
> are only sent while you're signed in.
>
> **Upgrading is normal.** Install the new APK over the top on Android, or run the installer on
> Windows. Your games and saves stay put.
>
> **Repatch once for the Montezuma music fix.** On Android it happens automatically the first time
> you launch each game. On Windows, press **Repatch** on the game's page, or start WPR with
> `--repatch-installed` to do your whole library.
>
> **Your old gamertag setting is gone.** Games now use your hub gamertag when you're signed in and
> a guest name when you're not, so the gamertag and picture fields have left both settings pages.
> Any scores you post as a guest go up under your account when you sign in.
