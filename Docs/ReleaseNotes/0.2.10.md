### Phones with less memory

- **Windows Phone 8 games give memory back.** When a game frees a large block (a level's art, a
  loaded song) the memory goes back to the phone instead of staying with WPR until the game exits.
  On a Galaxy S24, Fruit Ninja (2013) gave back about 370 MB this way.
- **Copies inside Windows Phone 8 games are much faster**, which shortens their loading screens.
- **Smaller textures.** On phones that have to unpack compressed textures (all of them on Vulkan),
  the most common kind now takes half the memory. Very smooth gradients can show slight banding.
- **Games stop thinking they are out of memory.** WPR used to report its own memory use as the
  game's, so some games cut back as if they were over the phone's limit.
- **WPR tidies its memory when Android asks**, for example when you switch away from a game.
- **Much less garbage collection** in Windows Phone 8 games, which removes many of the short pauses
  you could feel as stutter.

### Sound

- **Music and effects keep playing during loading screens** in Windows Phone 8 games. A loading
  screen that drew nothing for a few seconds used to stop the sound; it now plays on, as on a
  phone. N.O.V.A. 3's campaign load is now free of gaps.
- **Sound keeps up better while the phone is busy drawing**: far fewer crackles in Asphalt 7 races,
  and FIFA 13's match sound is fed as often as a phone fed it.
- **Music that starts with a long track no longer freezes the game** while it loads.

### Windows Phone 8 games

- **FIFA 13** plays a match. The pitch drew as a dark grid, players had no heads or arms, and the
  match load could run a phone out of memory.
- **Shadow Fight 2** plays its tutorial and the first fight, at 60 fps on a Galaxy S24, with music
  and sound. It used to freeze on the first tap or show a black screen.
- **Where's My Perry?** keeps your progress and settings between launches, and opening settings or
  pressing Play no longer crashes it.
- **Asphalt 7** closes straight away from its Exit button, also right after a race.

### Windows Phone 7 games

- **Skulls of the Shogun** connects to its async servers and plays "Skulls Anywhere" games against
  other WPR Hub players.
- **AE Roundy POP** plays.
- **Doodle Jump** is portrait again, and its game-over name box no longer stops the game when no
  keyboard box can be shown.

### Known issues

- **FIFA 13 matches play faster than they should.** Windows Phone 8 games run at full frame rate
  here, which makes Asphalt 7 smoother, but FIFA moves its match a fixed step per frame and was
  made for 24 frames a second.
- **Assassin's Creed Pirates** can load slowly after Play.
- **Unity games for Windows Phone 8** still show a black screen.
- **Halo** still draws some objects as black shapes.
- **Application bars** (the row of round buttons at the bottom of some Silverlight pages) still aren't
  drawn.
- Tested on Windows and a Galaxy S24. Not tested on a Mali-based phone.

> **Upgrading.** Install the new APK over the top on Android, or run the installer on Windows. Your
> games, saves and sign-in stay put. Installed games update themselves the first time you start them,
> so each one's first launch takes a little longer.
