### Geometry Dash

Tested with version 1.9.3 on Windows and a Galaxy S24.

- **It runs**, at 60 fps on the S24: menus, the level list, Stereo Madness and Back On Track, dying
  on the spikes and trying again, "New best!".
- **The screen**: only a stretched corner of the menu used to show, and taps landed well short of
  what you aimed at. The game now gets a drawing area of the size it asks for, as on a phone, so both
  the picture and your taps line up.
- **Backgrounds, ground and the player**: these were black. The game's graphics library never
  uploaded its textures because WPR answered one of its questions about them wrongly. Other games
  built the same way (Windows Phone 8 games that draw with OpenGL ES through ANGLE) may show textures
  that were missing before, too.
- **Music**: the menu music played a few notes, jumped back and played them again, moving on at
  about a sixth of its speed. Now it plays straight through.

### Fixes

- **Windows Phone 8 games that use FMOD for sound** (Geometry Dash, and others built on the same
  engine) were handed a larger sound buffer than they asked for. FMOD sizes its own mixing from the
  buffer it asked for, so it ended up replaying old sound. WPR now gives each game the buffer it asks
  for and keeps its own extra margin for slow frames out of sight.
- **Asphalt 7** still fills the screen in races, which use a smaller drawing area of their own.

> **Upgrading.** Install the new APK over the top on Android, or run the installer on Windows. Your
> games, saves and sign-in stay put. Nothing needs reinstalling.
