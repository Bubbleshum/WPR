**A small fix for WPR Hub sign-in on Android.**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 🔑 | **Sign-in no longer says "WPR Hub is not available in this copy of WPR"** when you come back from approving the key in your browser |

Signing in sends you to your browser to approve the link key. While you were there, Android
could close WPR in the background to free memory. When you came back, it reopened WPR straight on
the sign-in screen, which skipped the part of start-up that connects to the hub, so sign-in had
nothing to talk to. The same shortcut also left notifications, vibration and soundtrack conversion
switched off until you went back to the Start screen. Every WPR screen now finishes that start-up
itself, whichever one Android opens first.

> **Upgrading is normal.** Install the new APK over the top. Your games, saves and sign-in stay put.
> Nothing needs repatching. Windows is unchanged in this release.
