### Windows Phone 8 games

Games that bring their own engine now run it as shipped. WPR supplies what that engine asks the
phone for, rather than swapping in a stand-in. Gravity Guy 2 draws through its own copy of SharpDX
on a real Direct3D 11, and Microsoft's Mahjong, Minesweeper and Solitaire run their own engine
behind their Silverlight menus.

Managed Windows Phone 8 games (about 40 in our test library) now reach Xbox Live through WPR Hub:
you're signed in as your hub player, and achievements and leaderboards work as they do for native
games.

Fruit Ninja (2013), Jetpack Joyride and Halo used to stop with "guest thread exited and no thread
is runnable", the most common Windows Phone 8 crash report. Fruit Ninja and Jetpack Joyride now play
from their menus through a round and its results screen, fill the screen, and remember your best
score and progress between launches. They sign in to Xbox Live as your WPR Hub player, with
achievements and leaderboards. Halo plays on.

Windows Phone 8 games also run more evenly on phones: they no longer slow down the longer you play,
and a stutter every couple of seconds is gone.

### Windows Phone 7 and Silverlight games

- **AE Fruit Slash** saves, plays and goes back properly. On phones it no longer crashes after a lot
  of slashing, and it starts on Android, where it used to fail at launch.
- **iRunner**'s store, settings and saves work.
- **Need for Speed Undercover** and **The Sims 3** stand upright on Windows. They draw their landscape
  picture turned into a portrait screen and expect you to turn the phone; the window now turns itself
  when a game does that, and clicks line up with what you see. NFS also no longer freezes on OpenGL.
- **Fox News** shows its feed through its own list control. **YouTube** gets past start-up.
- **SPY Mouse** opens a level when you press Play, instead of an empty screen.
- **Contract Killer** no longer stops at its Xbox login.
- **Toolkit gestures**: games that ship the Windows Phone Toolkit now run its own gesture code
  instead of a WPR stand-in. Menu taps in Microsoft Minesweeper and Sudoku, which used to do
  nothing, now work. Hold, drag and pinch in play haven't been tested yet.
- Lists, pivots, clipping, skewed drawing and inline pictures in rich text are drawn properly, and
  pages with an application bar no longer fail to open.

### WPR Hub

- **Leaderboards have names.** Windows Phone 8 games send each board's real name. Other boards get
  a readable one like "Best score", and boards already on the hub are renamed.
- **Installs fill in a game's details** on the hub: name, publisher and icon, when the hub doesn't
  have them yet.
- **Game invites and notifications.** Invite a friend to a game from a conversation. On Android, new
  messages, invites, friend requests and achievements can pop up as notifications. Each can be
  turned off in settings.
- **Retired online services can be served by the hub.** Pool Pro Online 3's lobby is the first.
- **Fewer false crash reports.** A game Android closed on purpose, for example while an app was being
  installed, is no longer reported as a crash.

### Known issues

- **YouTube** reaches its first page but shows no videos: the 2013 YouTube service it asks no
  longer answers.
- **Jetpack Joyride** still has a slight hitch now and then on phones, and a short pause when a run
  ends while the game saves.
- **Halo** still draws some objects as black shapes.
- **Application bars** (the row of round buttons at the bottom of some Silverlight pages) aren't drawn
  yet.
- **Not tested on a Mali phone.** Phone testing was on a Galaxy S24 only.

> **Upgrading is normal.** Install the new APK over the top on Android, or run the installer on
> Windows. Your games, saves and sign-in stay put. Installed games are updated automatically the
> first time you start them, so each game's first launch takes a little longer.
