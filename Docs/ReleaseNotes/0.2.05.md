### Plants vs. Zombies on OpenGL

Plants vs. Zombies prepares its pictures while it loads, by drawing each one in the background
while the game shows its loading screen. Vulkan and Windows' own graphics (Direct3D) don't mind
which part of the game draws. OpenGL does: only one part of a program can talk to it at a time,
and the game's background loading wasn't that part. On Android that crashed the game a few
seconds in. On Windows with OpenGL it froze on the PopCap logo.

WPR now passes anything a game draws in the background over to the part that is allowed to talk to
OpenGL, and makes sure the two don't end up waiting on each other. Nothing changes on Vulkan or
Direct3D.

This matters mainly on phones using OpenGL. That's either because you picked it under
settings → graphics, or because WPR switched to it after Vulkan failed on your phone.

### Known issues

- **Not yet tested on a Mali phone.** The crash was reported from a realme phone with a Mali
  graphics chip. The fix was tested on the Android emulator and on Windows, both on OpenGL, but not
  on that phone itself.

> **Upgrading is normal.** Install the new APK over the top on Android, or run the installer on
> Windows. Your games, saves and sign-in stay put. Your installed games are updated automatically
> the first time you start them, which takes a moment longer than usual.
